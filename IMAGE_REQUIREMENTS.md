## Recommended Free Stock Photo Sources

1. **Pexels** (pexels.com)
   - Free to use commercially
   - Good selection of African/Nigerian content
   - Download high resolution

2. **Unsplash** (unsplash.com)
   - Free high-quality images
   - Attribution appreciated but not required
   - Great for educational settings

3. **Pixabay** (pixabay.com)
   - Free images and videos
   - No attribution required
   - Good for diverse content

4. **African Stock Photo** (africanstockphoto.com)
   - Specialized in African imagery
   - Some free, some paid
   - Authentic Nigerian content

## How to Add Images

1. Download images from stock photo sites
2. Rename appropriately (e.g., `hero-students.jpg`, `junior-school.jpg`)
3. Save to: `/Users/mac/Documents/GitHub/Blossom/src/assets/images/`
4. Update image imports in component files
5. Test in browser to ensure proper display
